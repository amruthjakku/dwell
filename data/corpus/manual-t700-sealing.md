## 6.1 Traysealer sealing principle

The T 700 seals pre-filled trays with a lidded film under modified atmosphere. The seal die is product-specific: a die for a 190 × 140 mm tray will not seal a 227 × 178 mm tray correctly even though both fit the machine.

Seal failures on this machine are almost always die-specific. If one tray format fails and others run clean, the die or its insert is the cause.

## 6.2 Seal die and insert replacement

Seal dies are mounted on a quick-release carriage. Wear inserts (the rubber backing that provides even pressure) should be replaced when the seal shows a visible thin band.

Replacement insert: [P:107110240] seal insert, 190 × 140 mm, and [P:107110258] seal insert, 227 × 178 mm.

## 6.3 Gas flushing and residual oxygen

Target residual oxygen is below 0.5% for red meat and below 2% for bakery. High residual oxygen with correct gas pressure points to a leaking gas lance seal or a die that is not seated flat.

Gas lance O-rings are a consumable: [P:107110366] O-ring set, pack of 20.

## 6.4 Sealing temperature and dwell for traysealers

Traysealer film is thinner than thermoformer film and needs less dwell. Default dwell is 1.2 s at 145 °C for 60 µm PET/PE lidding film. Raising temperature to fix a cold seal usually wrinkles the film instead — check die flatness first.

## 6.5 Tray jam and transport faults

Tray jams are reported as alarm 31. The most common cause is a worn infeed guide rail allowing trays to skew. Check rail parallelism before adjusting the transport drive.
