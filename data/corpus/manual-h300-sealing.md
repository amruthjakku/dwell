## 7.1 Sealing station overview

The sealing station closes the upper film against the lower web using a heated seal bar. Sealing pressure is generated pneumatically and monitored by pressure sensor B14. The station only releases the transport cycle when the seal pressure has been held for the full dwell time.

Alarm conditions at this station are reported as codes 40 through 49. The most common cause of a non-sealing machine is contamination or wear on the seal bar PTFE cover, not a failure of the pressure regulator.

## 7.2 Seal bar — inspection and replacement

Inspect the PTFE cover of the seal bar at every shift change. Replace the seal bar when the cover is torn, discoloured black, or has a visible groove deeper than 0.3 mm.

Replacement procedure: release seal pressure, isolate the compressed air supply, remove the two M6 retaining screws at each end of the bar, withdraw the bar forward out of the holder, fit the new bar with the part number facing the operator side, and re-tension to 8 Nm.

Replacement part: [P:105420118] seal bar 320 mm, and [P:105420126] PTFE cover set (pack of 5). After replacement, run three empty cycles before production.

## 7.3 Seal pressure and dwell settings

Seal pressure below 4.5 bar will not produce a gas-tight seam. Default dwell time is 1.8 s for 80 µm PA/PE film and 2.4 s for 200 µm film. If seams leak only at the corners, suspect uneven pressure across the bar rather than insufficient total pressure — check the four pressure equalising bolts.

Do not raise seal pressure above 6.5 bar to compensate for a worn bar. This deforms the bar and is not covered by warranty.

## 7.4 Seal seam leak diagnosis

A seam that leaks but is visually intact indicates either insufficient dwell time, contaminated sealing surfaces, or film temperature outside the process window. Test with the dye penetration method described in §9.4 before changing any settings.

If one side of the seam leaks consistently, the bar is misaligned. Alignment tolerance is ±0.2 mm over the full bar length.

## 7.5 Sealing station temperature control

The seal bar is heated by two cartridge heaters controlled in closed loop by thermocouple T1. A deviation of more than 8 °C from setpoint for longer than 30 seconds raises alarm 44.

If alarm 44 repeats after a thermocouple replacement, check the heater cartridge resistance — a value above 96 Ω indicates an ageing cartridge. Replacement part: [P:105420204] cartridge heater 230 V, 400 W.
